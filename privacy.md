# Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

## Who we are

iLang Inc. ("we", "us", "iLang") is a federally incorporated company in Canada. We operate ilang.ai and all associated products and services.

## What we collect

Site usage statistics only. ilang.ai uses Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages are read. Google Analytics sets cookies and receives your IP address, browser and device type, and the pages you visit; Google processes this data under its own privacy policy. We do not ask for or store names, email addresses or payment details, require no registration, and use no advertising identifiers. You can block Google Analytics with your browser's tracking protection.

## Interactive services

When you use interactive features such as the prompt structuring tool on ilang.ai or the AI See vision proxy (i.ilang.ai), your input is sent to third-party AI model providers (such as Anthropic, OpenAI, or Google) for processing. We do not store, log, or use submitted content for any purpose, including model training.

## Third-party services

When our products connect you to third-party services for booking, purchasing, or other actions, any data you provide is handled entirely by those third parties under their own privacy policies. We do not access or store your personal data, payment details, or documents.

## Affiliate links

Affiliate links may be used to support our free services. These links contain a partner identifier only. No personal information is transmitted through affiliate links.

## Open-source software

Our open-source tools (iLang protocol, AutoCode, Imprint, etc.) run entirely on your local machine or in your AI conversation window. They do not phone home, transmit data, or require network access.

## Children

Our services are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

## Changes

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent revision.
