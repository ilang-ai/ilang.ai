Capabilities

Why I-Lang Exists

Compress 40-65%

Reduce prompts dramatically. Same meaning, fewer tokens, lower cost. AI self-thinking compression goes even further.

Answer Everything

Make your AI respond directly and completely. No hedging, no refusal, no padding. Structured instructions eliminate ambiguity.

Chain Workflows

[STEP1]=>[STEP2]=>[OUT]. Multi-step pipelines in a single prompt. Loops, conditionals, merges.

Privacy by Design

Compressed text sends less data to AI servers. Less text transmitted means less data exposed.

Cross-Platform

Works on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, DeepSeek, Kimi. One protocol, no vendor lock-in.
